# Bidwall: Buyers, Framings, Pricing and GTM (desk research memo)

Date: 2026-10-04. Every factual claim cites a URL and source date. **[Inference]** marks our own reasoning. Figures labelled "team data" come from Bidwall's own mainnet-fork and quote work, not from outside sources.

## Summary

- **The risk Bidwall addresses is now public and named.** Aave V4's Base Equities Hub went live on 2026-09-25. Its risk provider, LlamaRisk, says outright that USDC lenders are exposed to a weekend price gap. The Chainlink feeds hold their last value from Friday 8pm to Sunday 8pm ET, and LlamaRisk's model assumes liquidators finish within five minutes of the market reopening ([CryptoSlate, 2026-09-27](https://cryptoslate.com/aave-stock-token-loans-expose-usdc-lenders-to-weekend-price-gap/)). TokenLogic's Aave Monad proposal sets collateral factors from the 99th-percentile historical weekend gap ([Crypto Times, 2026-09-29](https://www.cryptotimes.io/2026/09/29/aave-proposes-monad-v4-hub-for-tokenized-equity-lending/)). The ecosystem currently answers this risk with low LTVs. Bidwall offers a second answer: committed exit liquidity.
- **Lenders are competing on LTV.** Jupiter Lend advertises xStock LTVs of 65–75% and "10% higher liquidation thresholds vs other lending platforms" ([Jupiter docs, accessed 2026-10-04](https://jupiter-support.mintlify.app/user-docs/earn/lend/borrow/xstocks); [@jup_lend on X, via search snippet, undated](https://x.com/jup_lend/status/2057249202057707647)). Kamino caps MSTRx/CRCLx/HOODx at 30% (team data). **[Inference]** A product that safely raises LTV is a competitive lever for Kamino and its curators, not only a safety feature.
- **The closest precedent is a market maker that already bundles "committed liquidator" with curation.** On Flowdesk's Morpho SPYx vault, liquidations are "handled by Flowdesk, leveraging our existing liquidity onchain" ([Flowdesk, 2026-03-30](https://www.flowdesk.co/insights/flowdesk-agora-xstocks-launch-ausd-equity-strategy-vault-on-morpho)). Bidwall turns that bilateral, trust-based arrangement into an escrowed, non-cancellable onchain commitment on Kamino.
- **Who pays first.** **[Inference]** The best first payer is a curator whose yield or LTV competitiveness depends on xStock collateral: Sentora for the Kraken xStocks Vaults, plus Kamino's own xStocks market. Market makers are the natural capital providers. The borrower pays indirectly through a rate spread.
- **Recommended pitch framing:** a committed exit for tokenized collateral, together with a monitor that measures the liquidation-depth gap. Use the monitor to find demand and the bid to monetise it.

## 2. Framings, ranked

1. **Committed exit for xStock collateral, sold to curators as "higher safe LTV" (bid plus monitor).** This is the best fit with what is built: the settlement path already works against Kamino's real program. The demand signal is concrete: curators publicly own liquidation-threshold risk (Sentora), and LTV is a competitive axis (Jupiter vs Kamino). Pitch line: _"Flowdesk does this bilaterally on Morpho; we make it escrowed, non-cancellable and permissionless on Kamino."_
2. **(i) Liquidation-depth risk monitor for curators and risk teams.** This has the lowest adoption friction and needs no capital. The repo already contains a market risk monitor. LlamaRisk's depth figures are "dated snapshot[s]", e.g. roughly $0.27–1.08M per token at 2% impact as of Sept 17 ([CryptoSlate, 2026-09-27](https://cryptoslate.com/aave-stock-token-loans-expose-usdc-lenders-to-weekend-price-gap/)), so a live weekend-depth monitor fills a visible gap. Its weakness is that analytics is a thin business, since Chaos Labs, LlamaRisk and Allez Labs already sell risk services. Its value is as the wedge and the evidence engine for #1.
3. **(ii) Weekend liquidity reservation sold to the issuer.** Kraken/Backed already pays growth incentives, so "reserve $X of USDC bids per ticker each weekend" is an easy budget line **[Inference]**. It is weaker than #1 because the issuer does not control LTV; it only makes the case to lenders easier.
4. **(v) Generalised "committed buyer" for illiquid collateral (LSTs, LP tokens, tokenized credit with T+N redemption).** This is the biggest TAM and the right vision slide. Colosseum has already backed ODL, an "RWA platform for liquidator funds" in Cohort 5 ([Solana Compass, 2026-06-29](https://solanacompass.com/news/colosseum-admits-21-startups-to-its-5th-accelerator-cohort-drawn-from-the-frontier-hackathon-and-eternal-sprint)). That validates the narrative and also means there is a sibling company Colosseum already knows. Too broad as the opening pitch.
5. **(iii) Borrower-side "protected loans".** Morpho Pre-Liquidations show borrowers will opt into liquidation customisation (Morpho, 2025-02, above). It needs the lender to honour a higher LTV for protected positions, though, which means a Kamino market/reserve change. Better framed as the result of #1 than as a standalone product.
6. **(iv) Issuer-backed redemption at liquidation.** Conceptually strongest, because the issuer holds the shares. It needs legal and ops changes at Backed and depends on weekday redemption windows, so it cannot solve weekends because the underlying venue is closed **[Inference]**. Not hackathon-feasible.

## 3. Pricing and economics

**Comparable price points**

- **Market-maker retainers.** GSR's Stake DAO proposal reportedly asked $100k setup plus $20k/month plus a $1M BTC/ETH loan ([Spark comparison, 2026](https://www.spark.money/tools/crypto-market-maker-comparison); primary: [Stake DAO forum](https://gov.stakedao.org/t/gsr-market-making-proposal/677), not independently verified). Retainers range from a few thousand dollars to six figures a month (same Spark source). The alternative structure is loan plus call option, priced with Black-Scholes ([Flowdesk, 2024-10-29](https://www.flowdesk.co/insights/crypto-market-making-retainer-vs-loan-call-model)).
- **Cover and insurance.** Nexus Mutual lists 2–10%/yr, or as low as about 0.12% for battle-tested protocols ([Coin Bureau, 2026](https://coinbureau.com/education/defi-insurance)). Sherlock coverage costs 2–3% of covered amount per year ([Delphi Digital](https://members.delphidigital.io/projects/sherlock)).
- **Backstop capital yield.** Aave Umbrella stakers earn the aToken yield plus safety incentives, with USDC/USDT vaults above 10% in total ([Aave Umbrella docs](https://aave.com/docs/aave-v3/umbrella); [Aave FAQ](https://aave.com/faq)).
- **OEV revenue splits.** Chainlink SVR on Aave launched at 65% Aave / 35% Chainlink for the first six months ([crypto.news, 2025-03-28](https://crypto.news/aave-integrates-chainlink-svr-to-recapture-oracle-related-mev/)). A recapture rate of about 73% was reported, roughly $16M on $675M of liquidations through early 2026, but that comes from search-summarised secondary reporting and is unverified ([Chaos Labs](https://chaoslabs.xyz/posts/svr-monitoring-platform)).
- **Liquidation bonuses as the provider's built-in discount.** Kamino reference ranges are 3–10% for majors and 5–25% for long-tail assets ([Kamino curator docs](https://kamino.com/docs/curators/markets/liquidations)). Aave's Base equities hub caps the bonus at 5.5% (CryptoSlate, above).

**A credible reservation-fee model [Inference]**

- **Treat the bid as an out-of-the-money put and price it that way.** If the strike is set at the price where the loan hits liquidation LTV, a 30%-LTV loan is deep OTM, so the put is cheap. Pushing LTV higher makes it more expensive. Quote fees as **bps per year on reserved notional, tiered by ticker volatility and strike distance**, for example 2–4% on SPYx/QQQx and 8–15% on single names. Calibrate against the Nexus/Sherlock 2–10% band and the provider's hurdle rate.
- **The provider earns three things:** (a) the reservation fee, (b) the liquidation bonus or discount on fills, and (c) yield on escrowed USDC if escrow can be routed into Kamino supply. That third item would sharply cut the net cost of carry and is worth a design note.
- **Who pays:** the curator vault (Sentora/Kraken), out of the extra APY created by the higher LTV. The pitch math is simple: if a reservation lets a vault run 10 points higher LTV and that adds more than the fee in net APY, the vault pays. The issuer can co-fund it as an incentive (like xPoints). Bidwall takes a protocol take-rate on fees and fills, in the SVR style (e.g. 10–20%).
- **Sanity check.** $2M reserved at 8% is $160k/yr. A single GSR-style retainer is $240k/yr plus setup, so issuers and curators already budget amounts of this size for liquidity.

## 4. Go-to-market

**What judges reward.** The pitch video is reviewed first and decides the shortlist. It should cover team, problem and user, plus "feedback or validation they have received from users, even if informal." Judges value "evidence that a team is solving a real problem for real users," and post-deadline progress updates help ([Colosseum blog, 2025-05-08](https://blog.colosseum.com/perfecting-your-hackathon-submission/)). World's Fair runs Sep 14 to Oct 12 and scores seven dimensions: Founder–Market Fit, Insight, Product + Execution, Market Size, Communication, Viability and Traction. It asks for "go-to-market strategy, demand validation, and plans for developing distribution." Winners can enter the accelerator with $250k ([Colosseum hackathon page, accessed 2026-10-04](https://colosseum.com/hackathon)).

**Recent comparables:**

- Cohort 5 (2026-06-29) includes ODL (RWA liquidator funds) and Senthos (structured products) (Solana Compass, above).
- Cypherpunk winners (2025-12-19) include Yumi Finance (DeFi) and Autonom (RWA) ([Colosseum blog](https://blog.colosseum.com/cypherpunk-winners-stamp-light-token-firedancer/)).
- **[Inference]** Pitches like ODL's, which offer infrastructure to a named set of counterparties, are fundable when they cite a specific buyer set.

**Superteam.** Chapters offer micro-grants of about $200–$10k, legal help and VC introductions ([Solana Compass, Superteam profile](https://solanacompass.com/projects/superteam)). Ask the local chapter for warm intros to Kamino, Sentora and Flowdesk BD rather than for a grant.

## 5. Recommendation

Position Bidwall as **"committed exit liquidity for tokenized collateral: the onchain, escrowed version of what Flowdesk does privately, letting Kamino curators run higher LTVs on xStocks without weekend bad-debt risk."** Lead the pitch with the now-public weekend-gap problem (Aave/LlamaRisk, Sept 2026) and the LTV gap between Kamino (30% on volatile names) and Jupiter Lend (65–75%). Show the working settlement against Kamino's real program, and show the risk monitor as the tool that measures where a bid is needed.

Name Sentora (Kraken xStocks Vaults) as the design-partner target, with market makers as capital providers and Kraken/Backed as an incentive co-funder. Price reservations as a volatility-tiered annual fee on reserved notional, paid by the curator vault out of the extra APY, with a protocol take-rate. Keep "any illiquid collateral (LSTs, LP tokens, T+N RWAs)" as the vision slide, not the headline. Be explicit that discovery interviews are in progress and that you will report results weekly.
